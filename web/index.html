FireGuard-RT

Sign in with your CoreIoT account to monitor and configure your fire alarm nodes.

  1. Sign in to CoreIoT the way you usually do, including Google or GitHub.
  2. Open Account, Security and press Copy JWT token.
  3. Paste it below.
Stay signed in longer

A JWT token alone lasts about 2.5 hours. To renew it automatically, also paste the refresh_token value from the CoreIoT tab: Developer tools, Application, Local storage.

Credentials go only to the CoreIoT server. The session is kept in this browser tab until you close it or sign out.
